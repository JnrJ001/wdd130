Favorite City

Accra, Ghana

Accra is my favorite city. It's Ghana's capital, on the Gulf of Guinea, and it mixes old and new. Independence Square and Jamestown are historic sites, the business districts are modern, and Makola is a busy market. The food is what I love most. You can find jollof, waakye, and grilled tilapia almost anywhere. Music, art, and fashion are big here, and people are friendly, so you feel welcome fast. I also like that the tech and creative scene is growing. Accra feels like a city that's still changing.
